It does not prescribe.

Enter your own names, amounts and times, then record what actually happened. PepLoom never recommends doses, combinations or injection sites, and it has no calculators. Follow your clinician’s instructions for treatment and dosing.

  • Your plan, in your wordsNames, amounts, units and times are the ones you enter.

Features

Everything you record, in one calm place.

Built natively for iPhone, in warm ivory and gold. Simple enough for a few taps a day, detailed enough to keep an honest history.

  • Schedules

    Daily or selected-weekday schedules with one or more times a day, exact amounts and explicit units. Pause, resume or change a schedule without rewriting past records.

  • Dose records

    Record the actual amount and time, or mark an entry as skipped. Correct a record later when you need to.

  • Body-map location Optional

    Pin where you injected on a front or back body illustration and review previously recorded locations. PepLoom describes your records; it doesn’t recommend sites.

  • Stacks

    Group your own schedules into stacks, in the order you choose, and see their recent records together.

  • History & insights

    Filter history by peptide, stack, outcome and date. See daily records and per-peptide counts, with taken and skipped kept separate.

  • Journal

    Quick check-ins with a mood rating and an optional note, kept alongside your history.

  • Manual inventory

    A simple record of your vials, remaining amounts and expiry dates. You update it yourself; logging a dose never changes it.

  • Reminders

    Optional local notifications for the schedules you choose. Reminder text never shows peptide names or amounts.

  • Widgets

    Small and medium Home Screen widgets show how many entries are left to log today and your recording streak, without names or amounts.

Privacy

Private by design.

Your records belong to you. They stay on your iPhone unless you choose to export them or turn on optional private backup.

  • Stored on your iPhone

    Records live in PepLoom’s private app storage with iOS file protection, and are not included in iCloud or computer backups.

  • No ads, no tracking

    No advertising identifier and no analytics or tracking SDKs. Your records are never sold or used for marketing.

  • Yours to export

    Export everything as JSON or your dose history as CSV, restore a backup, or erase it all, even without an active plan.

  • Optional private backup

    Sign in with Apple to keep a backup copy on PepLoom’s server. Nothing uploads until you choose. Encrypted in transit, not end-to-end encrypted.

PepLoom does not access Apple Health, your camera, contacts or location.
